# Spirit Level

A 3D spirit level in the style of the iPhone level. A phone sits on an oak board balanced on a ball joint, and its screen shows the level: two circles that merge when flat, a horizon line when it stands on an edge, and a green screen at 0°.

- Tap **Use my phone's tilt** on a phone and the level reads the phone's own motion sensors. The 3D phone copies how you hold it.
- Switches between flat (bullseye) and on-edge or upright (horizon) by itself.
- Tap the 3D phone to save an angle as a reference, then match it on another surface.
- Without sensors, drag to tilt the board or watch the demo.
- Everything is in `index.html`; 3D by three.js r128.

Live at https://w1zman.github.io/spirit-level/
